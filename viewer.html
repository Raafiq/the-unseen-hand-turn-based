The real headless sim, running in your browser. You command Team A. On your turn, click a highlighted tile to stage a move (nothing touches the sim yet — cancel is free), then click an enemy to strike from there: one command, one turn, −100 CT. Or End Turn to pay only for what you used. Every number in the Resolution preview — hit %, the facing arc that produced it, the exact damage, the CT price — is computed before you commit. Press Play enemy turn to watch the AI resolve one. Everything is deterministic from the seed — the AI advances on that click, never on a timer, so the same seed always replays the same battle.
