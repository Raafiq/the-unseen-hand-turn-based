# Variety / Chaos — `--chaos` / `--c`

> Source: Midjourney docs, **Variety** (`/hc/en-us/articles/32099348346765`).
> Pasted by the owner **2026-09-01**. Badged "This feature is supported in V8.2".
> `[OFFICIAL]`.

Called **Variety** on midjourney.com and **Chaos** in the parameter.

> "Chaos ... lets you add more variety to the image results you get from each
> prompt.
>
> By default chaos is set to **0**, and Midjourney creates four images based on
> your prompt. If you want each image to look more different, increase the chaos
> value.
>
> You can set the chaos value anywhere between **0 and 100**, but keep in mind that
> higher values mean the images can be quite different and **may not stick as
> closely to your prompt**, giving you unpredictable results."

`--c #` at the end of a prompt, or a default in the settings panel.

## For this project

**Leave at 0.** A style probe compares three *prompts* against each other; the
spread that matters is between the grids, not inside one. Raising chaos loosens
each grid's grip on its own prompt, which is the opposite of what you are trying to
read.
