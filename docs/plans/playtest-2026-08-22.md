# Playtest — 2026-08-22, first hands-on pass

**What this was:** the game driven in a real browser, every screen captured, by the agent
that built it. **What it was NOT:** a newcomer playtest. I know the systems, so nothing
here is evidence about discoverability, difficulty or fun. Those remain untested.

Screens: `visual-artifacts/playtest/*.png`. Capture spec: `e2e/playtest-capture.spec.ts`.

## Two attempts at hand-play failed, and that is a finding

I tried twice to play battle one by keyboard and both runs ended with the AI finishing the
fight. The cause was my harness, not the game: **the information a player uses to act —
the highlighted move range, the target tiles — is drawn on a canvas a script cannot read.**
The game page exposes no "which tiles are targets" seam (`viewer-api.ts` gives the engine
viewer one; `game-api.ts` does not).

Consequence worth recording: **no automated test can check that what is DRAWN matches what
is CLICKABLE.** Every existing browser test drives the seam, not the picture. That gap is
exactly what let the bug below ship.

## Bugs found — both by looking at a screenshot, both fixed

1. **Every unit on the board was the same grey.** `drawUnit` read `UNIT_META`, a table
   keyed by the four *demo* unit ids (`knight`, `archer`, `brawler`, `mage`). The
   campaign's ids are `blue-vance`, `red-brigand-1`, … so every lookup missed and friend
   and foe were painted one fallback colour. `game.ts` had a `TEAM_COLOR` table and passed
   it only to the side panels. **A player could not tell their party from the enemy by
   looking at the grid.** Fixed: `draw` takes a `unitColor` mapping; the game page supplies
   team colour. Test: a recording canvas context asserts two teams reach two colours.
2. **The turn log printed internal ids.** "hit red-brigand-1 −137", directly under a
   timeline chip reading "Brigand". The actor was resolved through `look()`; the target,
   which lives inside the action *string*, was not. Fixed and tested with a fixture whose
   ids deliberately do not look like names — the demo's ids (`knight`) are already
   readable, so a test written against those would pass either way.

Both were invisible to 716 passing tests, because no test read pixels or rendered text.

## Observations — real, not fixed, not green-lit

- **The final battle's prep screen shows Vance with one command: Attack.** Eight of the
  nine rows in his Knight tree are tagged "no effect yet". This is the known
  `battle-skill` exclusion (user decision, 2026-08-16), but seeing it at battle five —
  208 AP banked, nothing to spend it on that does anything — is worse than reading about it.
- **Nothing tells you a learn row is a passive.** "Hp Boost" sits in the same list as
  actions with no marker saying it must then be equipped in the Support slot.
- **The weapon dropdown defaults to "Unarmed"** while the party owns nine weapons by
  battle five. Nothing prompts the swap; "Unarmed" reads as a state, not an invitation.
- **The Bulwark trait sits unchecked at the last battle.** Free power, no nudge.
- **Turn one offers no attack** — the enemy starts outside everyone's move range. Correct
  for a tactics game, but it means the first thing a new player does is walk.
- **Layout:** a large empty column under the Job box on the briefing; the board is small
  in a large canvas.

## What would actually settle the open questions

A person who has not seen this repo, playing `/game.html` start to finish, timed, thinking
aloud. Nothing in this repository can produce that.
