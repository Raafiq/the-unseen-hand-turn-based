# ADR-0019 — The reaction slot gets real effects, and the credit has to follow the reactor

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-18
- **Supersedes / amends:** amends **ADR-0014** (diversity-gate manifest: `bld-counter-wall` EXCLUDED → MEASURABLE; `N` unchanged at 7). Second instance of the defect class **ADR-0017** named.
- **Owner docs:** `docs/01` §9a, `docs/02` §2, `docs/05` §2/§2a/§3, `docs/06` AC-E2, `docs/10` §4, `src/sim/CLAUDE.md`

## Context

ADR-0017 found that `build.ts` projected **action** abilities only, so three of the five
chassis slots were validated at equip time and then discarded. It fixed one of them.

The other two stayed dead, and the cost was already on the books:

- **7 of 15 shipped builds** wore a reaction that did nothing.
- The diversity gate carried `bld-counter-wall` in `EXCLUDED` with the tag
  *"reaction-as-live modeling (Counter is a passive reaction)"* — a build whose entire
  identity is the slot that was switched off.
- `docs/02` §2 described a five-slot chassis as a locked decision while two of the five
  were decorative.

This is the class root `CLAUDE.md` states as **a capability that validates its input and
then discards it reads as working**. It is worth restating that it recurred *inside the
same file*, one slot over, after being written down.

## Decision

**1. The equipped reaction applies real effects, authored as data on the ability.**

New `src/sim/reaction.ts` mirrors `support.ts`: a `ReactionEffectSchema`
(`{ kind: "counter" | "preemptive" }`), a projected `ReactionStateSchema`, and a
`DEFERRED_REACTION_EFFECTS` manifest. `AbilitySchema` gains an optional `reactionEffect`
— additive and optional, so **`CONTENT_SCHEMA_VERSION` does not bump**.

`UnitState` gains `reaction` (**schema v10 → v11**), projected at build time so a replay
stays registry-free (ADR-0010/ADR-0011).

**2. The trigger chance is Brave, and is not authorable.** `docs/01` §4 already says
Brave sets reaction trigger odds, and every Brave-based reaction in FFT uses the raw stat.
A per-ability multiplier would make the first authored number the de-facto spec.

**3. Reach is the reactor's own basic-attack range**, asked of `grid.ts`'s
`inAbilityRange` rather than re-derived. A ranged physical blow therefore draws no answer
from a melee unit — which is a real tactical lever, and the discriminating negative that a
"reactions always answer" implementation fails.

**4. There is no reaction chain, structurally.** The counter-swing is resolved **inline**
in `tryReaction`, never routed back through `resolveAttack`. The invariant is a property
of the call graph, not a flag someone has to remember to pass.

**5. The reaction draw is CONDITIONAL, at a declared slot.** Per (attacker, defender)
blow: hit roll (always) → reaction trigger (only when the equipped reaction's condition
holds) → the reaction's swing hit roll (only when it fired). A unit with no reaction
consumes exactly the draws it consumed before this ADR, which is why the frozen golden
moved by representation alone — it was regenerated by two mechanical field edits and then
matched the engine byte for byte, which is the classification rather than a re-paste.

One deliberate deviation: **the hit roll is drawn even when Hamedo then cancels the blow.**
FFT's "the attack never happens" would skip it. Keeping the draw unconditional preserves
"the hit roll is always drawn" as an invariant instead of a special case, and the discarded
result is unobservable in the outcome.

**6. A fired reaction accounts for itself, credited to the REACTOR.** This is the half
that nearly shipped invisible.

`hpDiffEvent` credits a whole-state HP diff to the acting unit, and deliberately never
credits a unit for its own HP loss. A counter's damage lands on the **attacker** — the
diff's own source. So the counter would have fired correctly, changed the fight, and scored
**zero** on every measurement the project has: the gate's `inBand`, `signatureActionsLanded`,
the whole diversity count. `bld-counter-wall` would still have measured as contributing
nothing, and its blocker tag would have read as unretired.

Each fired reaction now emits its own `ResolutionEvent` under the reaction ability's id.
`docs/05` §2a states the general rule: **any effect that moves HP on behalf of someone
other than the acting unit accounts for itself rather than riding the diff.**

**7. Reraise, Retribution and Gilgame Heart stay DEFERRED, each with a named blocker.**
Reraise is the interesting one: nothing in the engine can revive a downed unit — every
resolver refuses a target at 0 HP, `punch-art.revive` / `white-magic.raise` are `heal`
formulas that cannot reach one, and the crystal countdown has no reverse. Adding a
`reraise` kind to the enum without that mechanic would be the dead-slot defect one level up.

**8. The MOVEMENT slot stays inert — by SCOPE, and it is recorded as such.**
`DEFERRED_MOVEMENT_EFFECTS` names all four movement abilities and splits them into SCOPE
(nothing blocks it) and BLOCKED (the mechanic does not exist), asserted by a partition test
plus an **A/B proving equipping one changes nothing**. That A/B is the part that matters:
when the slot is woken, it is the test that must go red. A manifest alone can rot.

The scope reason is measurable, not aesthetic: **8 of 15 builds equip `steal.move-plus-2`**,
so waking it hands +2 Move to more than half the roster at once — a tempo change on a roster
whose seventh identity sits exactly on the viability floor. It deserves its own measurement.

## Consequences

**The variety score does not move. It stays at 7 against a release bar of 8.**

`bld-counter-wall` is now MEASURABLE and clears **6/6** phys maps with its signature landed
on every one — its blocker is genuinely retired. It contributes **zero** new identities,
because `punch-art.` is already carried by `bld-faithzero-monk`. A second *carrier*, not a
new identity; the same collapse `bld-spellblade` and `bld-arcane-artillery` show on
`black-magic.`.

**A retired blocker does not owe the count a point.** Saying so plainly is cheaper than the
alternative, which is a future session reading "the reaction slice landed" and assuming the
count moved with it.

The gate now asserts the collapse **in both directions**, because the old sweep rested on an
assumption that quietly stopped holding — *one credited build per identity*. Dropping **all**
carriers of a prefix must cost one identity; dropping **one of two** must cost none. Without
the second half, "these two builds are one identity" would be an assumption the manifest
merely asserts in prose.

**The capability is real and moves real numbers, which had to be measured rather than
assumed.** An A/B over the whole gauntlet with the effects stripped moves **13 of 96 runs**:
the monk's `hpFraction` and signature-landed rise on Hamedo, the wizard's `candidateDamage`
rises 340 → 380 on Counter. The aggregate verdict (`pass`, `N`, `dominantBuilds`) is
unchanged, and an earlier probe that printed only those aggregates read "identical" — which
looked exactly like the dead slot this ADR exists to fix. **When a change should move
something, diff the rows, not the summary.**

`winsAllInBand` gains `bld-counter-wall` beside the monk: two builds now sweep every
{map × opposition} cell without outclassing the field. Surfaced for design review, not a
gate failure — but it is the second entry on a list that should not grow.

`bld-aggro-tank` stays EXCLUDED with a **re-measured** tag. It is no longer blocked from
being *measured* — it clears 6/6 too — but from being **distinct**: its gauntlet rows are
byte-identical to `bld-counter-wall`'s, the two builds differing only in raw HP (255 vs 242)
and trait order, neither of which mattered in any shipped run. An EXCLUDED tag is a
hypothesis; this one was re-derived rather than inherited.

**The viewer's silence became a lie the moment this shipped.** `preview.ts` correctly said
nothing about reactions while they were unmodeled. `ActPreview` now carries an optional
`counterRisk` — trigger odds, the counter-swing's own hit %, the exact damage, whether it is
lethal, and whether it cancels the act — absent whenever no reaction can trigger, including
when the act would kill the target first. The same audit found `status-on-hit` had been
sitting in the panel's "not modeled" list *after* the inflict path went live. That list is an
assertion and has to shrink as capabilities land (`docs/10` §4).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Wake reaction and movement together.** Doubles the balance surface in one slice, on a
  roster with no slack. Movement is recorded and handed off instead.
- **Let the counter ride the attacker's HP diff.** Simplest code, and it silently discards
  the reactor's contribution — the exact shape of the bug being fixed.
- **Add a `reraise` kind now.** There is no revive mechanic to hang it on.
- **Make the trigger chance authorable.** No design need; it would freeze the first number
  someone typed into the spec.
