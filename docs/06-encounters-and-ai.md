# 06 — Encounters & AI

**Why this is a first-class doc, not a QoL bullet:** customization only *means* something against encounters that test it. Encounter design is the **demand side** of the customization economy and the **primary balancer** (`docs/04` §2). The AI is the **test harness** that decides whether a build is actually viable.

---

## 1. Encounters are the balancer

A build is "viable" only relative to what it must beat. So:
- We maintain a **benchmark encounter suite** — the fixed set the success metric runs against (`docs/00`: ≥8 archetypes clear it within the efficiency band, none dominates).
- Balance passes **re-run the suite** after any `docs/02`/`docs/07` change. If one build clears everything at top efficiency, that's a balance bug surfaced by encounters, not spreadsheets.
- **Difficulty comes from smarter enemies and better-designed maps, not stat-inflation.** (The genre's most common complaint is "hard mode = bigger numbers.")

## 2. Encounter archetypes (design vocabulary)

Each archetype stresses a different axis of the player's builds:

| Archetype | What it tests | Design levers |
|---|---|---|
| **Skirmish (open field)** | Raw build efficiency, turn economy | Symmetric-ish forces, neutral terrain |
| **Siege / choke** | Positioning, AoE, ZoC-less flow | Height, walls, one approach |
| **Defend / survive N turns** | Sustain, control, Reraise | Waves, escalating pressure |
| **Escort / protect** | Mobility, healing, aggro management | Fragile ally with its own CT turns |
| **Assassinate boss** | Burst, disable, focus-fire | High-value target + guards |
| **Elevation puzzle** | Jump/Move/Teleport, ranged height bonus | Verticality, gaps, water |
| **Anti-cheese check** | Punishes dominant strategies | Mixed damage types, status immunity pockets, dispellers |
| **Attrition / no-heal** | Resource discipline, MP economy | Long map, scarce recovery, undead |

Story battles (from the narrative repo later) are authored instances of these; the engine is data-driven so they slot in without code (`docs/05` §6, `docs/08`).

> **Implementation status (P2 Slice 3; support-aware update 2026-08-03; survive-condition update 2026-08-03).** The benchmark suite authors the archetypes expressible today — Skirmish, Siege/choke, Assassinate, Elevation, Anti-cheese, Attrition (`data/encounters/`). **The `survive` win/lose condition has now LANDED** (`src/sim/condition.ts`): `survive {teamId, ticks}` `[ENHANCEMENT]` — a team wins by keeping ≥1 unit alive until the CT clock (`state.tick`) reaches `ticks`. NOTE the field is **`ticks` (the CT clock), NOT rounds/turns**: a unit acts roughly every ~(100/Speed) ticks, so "survive ~5 rounds" at the default Speed 5 is authored as `ticks: 100`. Tag rationale: the turn/CT-bounded objective *category* is baseline (FFT has protect-until / defeat-within-N), but a pure win-BY-survival *victory* is an enhancement (canonical in FFTA/FFTA2, not our PSX baseline). Pair a survive VICTORY with an `eliminateTeams{[teamId]}` defeat (the two are exact complements at the threshold tick — no spurious draw); the symmetric `survive`-as-DEFEAT is the **defeat-within-N-ticks** objective. Authoring: set `maxTurns` generously and `maxTicks` only slightly above `ticks` so the tick cap is binding and `timeout` stays a genuine error signal (the `survive.ticks <= maxTicks` refine is a coarse guard; the `maxTurns` decision-cap is dynamic and NOT statically guarded). **What has NOT landed yet:** a **Defend benchmark ENCOUNTER** wired into the gauntlet, and measurement of the **sustain identity** it would exercise — the condition exists, but no survive-objective encounter/build is in the diversity gate yet, so Defend is not yet a measured archetype (a later slice). **Support-aware body-screening has now LANDED** (`src/sim/ai.ts` `tryScreen`, Tier A): a sturdy unit with no in-range action **interposes** on the enemy's reachable path to a **mid-charge** ally (blocking the lane via `moveRange`'s occupancy). It is a **movement fallback only** — `compareCandidate`/the action path are untouched, so a screener with any in-range action still acts. **But it did NOT unblock the glass Summoner** (the target): the summon whiffs **zero** times (the old "charge-whiff" tag was empirically wrong — ADR-0014 amendment 2026-08-03); the real blocker is that the short-range summoner **advances into melee to reach cast range and is focus-fired mid-charge**, and reachability-screening can't wall a corner-seated glass caster with **2 fillers vs 3 bruisers** on multi-lane maps (only the choke-map `enc-the-breach` holds → **1/6**). So the summoner stays EXCLUDED and **`N` stays 5**; the screening ships as **latent capability the Escort archetype needs**, regressing nothing (live gate byte-identical — the mid-charge gate means the instant-cast Coven never triggers it, so the magic axis is untouched). **Heal-in-encounter partially landed:** the balance-probe now **triages** — it heals the lowest-effective-HP ally (AC-E3(b) mirrored onto allies, ADR-0014 amendment), so a healer with a wounded ally *in range* saves the dying one first. **What Escort / Defend still need to become MEASURED archetypes:** an Escort/Defend benchmark **encounter** in the gauntlet, and — for the Summoner specifically — a **survivability lever outside the 1-ply AI** (a tankier chassis / a faster-maturing or longer-range summon that lands before contact, or multi-body wall coordination / self-preservation movement — the last reopens magic-axis recalibration). Body-screening alone is not enough; don't re-attempt the Summoner via positioning tweaks.
>
> **AoE-resolution slice (post-Slice-3).** Area abilities now resolve against every appropriate unit in their box (`aoe` threaded through the combat projection; instant + charged, targeted foes/allies, one seeded roll per target in id order). **Instant** AoE (Geomancer) lands and is measured in-suite. **Charged** AoE (Summoner) resolves correctly (proved end-to-end with the real build/AI/harness) but is **not exercised by the current suite**: the glass caster is the lowest-HP unit, so the probe focus-fires it and it dies before its slow summon matures — its damage output is unmeasured, and `abilityUsage` counts the *issued* (then cancelled) command, not landed damage. This is the **same limitation family** — a slow-setup / glass-cannon archetype. **Empirically refined (2026-08-03, ADR-0014 amendment):** protect-the-enabler body-screening was built and is **insufficient** for the Summoner (it advances into melee to reach its short cast range, then dies mid-charge; screening can't wall it 2-vs-3 on multi-lane maps). Becoming measurable needs a **survivability lever outside the AI** — a tankier chassis / a faster-maturing or longer-range summon that lands before contact — not predictive charge-targeting (0 whiffs measured) and not positioning alone. Friendly-fire AoE (the anti-clump counterplay of §4) is a deliberate later slice; today's AoE is targeted (no friendly fire).

## 3. Threat / difficulty model

- **Threat budget per encounter:** enemy count × tier × support density, tuned to a target **time-to-kill** band (`docs/07`) — not to the player's level (no mandatory scaling treadmill).
- **Difficulty tiers** (player-selectable, `docs/04` §6) adjust AI aggression/coordination and threat budget, **not** raw stat multipliers first. Stat bumps are the last lever, not the first.
- **Telegraphing:** big enemy plays (charged nukes, incoming hard-disables) are visible via the same resolution-transparency the player gets (`docs/04` §3) — fair difficulty.

## 4. AI as the test harness for builds

The AI must be good enough that **counterplay actually happens** — otherwise "power with counterplay" is a lie. Target behaviors:

- **Positioning:** use facing (attack from side/rear), height, and safe tiles; avoid clustering into player AoE; respect its own charged-spell tile targeting.
- **Focus-fire:** concentrate on the highest-value / lowest-effective-HP target; finish units in the crystal window to deny revives; punish the exposed alpha-striker (`docs/03` #2 counterplay).
- **Counter the player's build:** prefer magic vs. high-evasion physical walls; use Silence vs. casters, Dispel vs. self-buffers, status that ignores Faith vs. low-Faith tanks; disable the enabler (the Haste-bot, the summoner) first.
- **Resource sense:** don't waste charged nukes on a unit that will walk away; use terrain/elements; retreat/reposition when losing tempo.
- **Threat assessment:** value disables and tempo, not just damage (mirror the player's tools).

**Determinism:** all AI decisions draw from the seeded RNG in declared order (`docs/05` §3) so battles stay reproducible and rewind-safe. Tie-broken decisions must be deterministic.

## 5. Anti-degenerate guarantees (encounter-side)

- **No mandatory grind:** encounters give bounded, non-RNG-treadmill rewards (`docs/07`); you never *must* farm randoms to progress.
- **Anti-cheese encounters** exist specifically to answer dominant strategies (mixed damage, immunity pockets, dispellers) — the structural check on stun-lock, evasion-stacking, and Blade-Grasp walls (`docs/04` §1, §5).
- **Stun-lock cap:** repeated hard-disables on the same unit hit diminishing returns (implemented as a status rule, `docs/05`), so AI *and* player are bounded.

## Acceptance Criteria (SDD-ready)

- **AC-E1 (benchmark exists):** A named benchmark suite covering the §2 archetypes SHALL exist and be runnable headlessly for balance verification.
- **AC-E2 (diversity gate):** On the benchmark suite, ≥ 8 `docs/03` archetypes SHALL clear within the efficiency band and no single build SHALL clear all encounters at top efficiency. **Phased (ADR-0014):** the greedy 1-ply probe can measure a *distinct identity* for **6** of the shipped archetypes today (`aim.`, `black-magic.`, `geomancy.`, `punch-art.`, `summon.`, `white-magic.` — `spellblade` and `arcane-artillery` share the `black-magic.` prefix, so those two collapse to one; the rest field as masked brawlers or need capabilities that don't exist — provoke, reaction-as-live). The CI gate enforces `distinctMeasurableArchetypes ≥ N` (**N=5 now — re-baselined twice on 2026-08-12, see the note at the end of this criterion**) **plus** a relative, threshold-free dominance ban (a build fails only if it clears all six gauntlet maps and no other measurable build clears any faster), with a committed `MEASURABLE`/`EXCLUDED` manifest naming each blocker. **Support-aware AI slice (landed, ADR-0014 amendment 2026-08-03):** a **heal-triage** refinement (the probe heals the lowest-effective-HP ally — AC-E3(b)'s focus rule mirrored onto allies — not the biggest overheal) shipped, and `bld-reraise-cleric` moved EXCLUDED→MEASURABLE as the **`white-mage`** identity (its *offensive* white-magic via `holy`; it was mis-excluded — only its unmodeled reaction/sustain identity was blocked). ⚠ **N=6 is not a durable floor:** **two** of the six prefixes are **contingent on MP being unenforced** — `white-magic.holy` (56 MP vs the cleric's 24) and now `summon.*` (14–30 MP vs the summoner's 24; the sim does not check MP yet). When MP enforcement lands both could regress and N could drop **6→4**; uniquely among the phased capabilities, MP enforcement *lowers* N. **`glass-summoner` moved EXCLUDED→MEASURABLE (ADR-0014 amendment 2026-08-05)** as the **`summoner`** identity via a range `[ENHANCEMENT]` (offensive `summon.*` h4→h6) that lets the glass caster open `summon.` from its backline instead of advancing into melee and dying mid-charge (the re-diagnosed *structural-survivability* blocker, not charge-whiff — 0 whiffs measured). `battle-cleric` (prefix collapse) stays EXCLUDED with its refined tag. **Multi-matchup opposition (landed, ADR-0014 amendment):** the gauntlet fields each candidate against an `OPPOSITIONS` set of distinct *threat* profiles — `phys` (the reference bruiser team) + `magic` (the Coven: instant Faith-scaling geomancy) — so threat, not just geometry, varies. Opportunity cost is real and *surfaced* per build (`losingMatchups`); the roll-call moves with every re-tune, so read it from a live `npm run state` rather than from this line — as of 2026-08-12 `reraise-cleric` and `glass-summoner` fold to magic while `terrain-geo`, `longshot` and `faithzero-monk` clear both axes (a non-uniform matrix). **Scope honesty:** the diversity count still keys on the `phys` **reference** opposition only (a build is *supposed* to lose to some threat, so requiring viability everywhere would collapse the count), so a magic regression is *surfaced* (`losingMatchups`/`noLosingMatchup`), not enforced — the sole hard fail stays relative dominance (now over `{maps × oppositions}`). The magic axis is a second distinct threat, **not** an isolated candidate-Faith test: shared Faith-50 filler allies + team-elimination + magnitude-focus targeting mean it rewards tempo/range as much as personal Faith (so the anti-mage build still shows a *team*-level magic loss — its personal resistance can't save its allies without support-aware positioning); the isolated Faith cliff is proved by a must-fail straddle test. Anti-convergence is thus *tested and surfaced*, not yet auto-enforced; `≥ 8` remains the release bar and `N` rises only as a new signature prefix (job/skillset) or those capabilities land. ⚠ **N RE-BASELINED TWICE ON 2026-08-12: 6 → 1 → 5.** Teaching the balance probe ADR-0015's move+act fold (one turn may move *and* act, settling at −100) dropped six of seven candidates just under `VIABLE_MIN_MAPS`=4, leaving one identity (`black-magic.`). **The follow-up diagnosis found the fold was not the operative cause (ADR-0016).** Every shipped build died to ONE basic attack — a 72-HP knight vs a 90-damage swing — so fights lasted 2–4 turns and were settled by turn order, making range, positioning, tempo and signature abilities all invisible. `docs/07` §3 had specified the intended pacing all along ("a squishy unit dies in ~1–2 committed actions; a tank in ~3–4") but nothing tested it, so the content missed it by 3–4× unnoticed. **The fix is content, symmetric, and applied to BOTH sides** — `raw.hp` re-authored across all 14 builds into the docs/07 band, now enforced by `docs/07` **AC-P6** (`src/sim/ttk.test.ts`). A second, dependent fix was forced by the first: `magicDamage` applies Faith at both ends, so at the shared Faith 50/50 every magic ability is quartered while physical ones are not — and geomancy's `power` had been authored on the physical scale, leaving a geomancer's spell (45) at half its own punch (80). The probe therefore punched, and `terrain-geo` won its maps *as a knight* (the masking class). Geomancy power ×~2.7 fixes it, and also restores the **magic threat axis**, which had gone toothless against 315-HP bodies. Measured after: `faithzero-monk` 6/6, `terrain-geo` 6/6, `longshot` 5/6, `glass-summoner` 5/6, `reraise-cleric` 4/6 → **N=5**, robust at 5–6 across a ±15% HP perturbation. **The one identity still missing is `black-magic.`**: `arcane-artillery` is too fragile to land four casts, and `bld-spellblade` is MASKED (a knight's MA 6 makes borrowed magic lose to its own swing) — asserted positively in `ttk.test.ts` so it cannot be forgotten. `geomancy.*` is BACK in the as-authored benchmark suite's required-skillset list, the deliberate update its exactly-zero pin was designed to force. `≥ 8` is unchanged as the release bar.
- **AC-E3 (AI counterplay):** Enemy AI SHALL demonstrably (a) attack from side/rear when reachable, (b) focus the lowest-effective-HP valid target, and (c) select damage/status types that counter the opposing unit's defenses, in scripted test scenarios.
- **AC-E4 (determinism):** Given a fixed seed and player command log, AI decisions SHALL be reproducible (draws from the seeded stream only).
- **AC-E5 (difficulty without inflation):** Raising a difficulty tier SHALL first change AI behavior/threat budget; raw stat multipliers SHALL be a bounded, secondary lever.
