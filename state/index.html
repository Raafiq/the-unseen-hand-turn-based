ARCH

How it's built

One rule governs the shape: the sim is pure and headless (ADR-0007). Determinism is a P0 invariant — a single seeded PRNG drives every roll in a declared order, so a battle is byte-for-byte reproducible from (seed, commands). That's what makes rewind, saves, and shareable build codes possible.

SPINE

The customization spine

The whole game is organized around one locked design decision (ADR-0001): depth comes from three customization axes, and success is measured in meaningfully different, viable builds — not in the number of options. Everything else is optional or deferred.

Respec is free and progress is permanent — learned abilities and masteries are never lost, loadout swaps cost nothing (ADR-0002). Experiments are safe by design; specialization still costs a generalization (the anti-convergence law).

GATE

The build-diversity gate

This is the pillar's safety net and the most recent work. A substitution gauntlet fields each reference build across the shipped maps against distinct threat profiles, and checks two things: enough distinct viable identities land their signature, and no build strictly outclasses the field. It fields two opposition axes — physical and magic — so a build can finally lose to something (real opportunity cost), not just win everywhere. NEXT

What's next

Several reference builds are still unmeasured because the greedy 1-ply probe can't exercise their identities yet — each is tagged in the manifest with the capability that unblocks it. The moves, by leverage:

01

Re-tune the gauntlet content against the folded turn  recommended

The move+act fold landed (2026-08-12): ai.ts now moves and acts in one command priced at −100 CT, so closers can finally execute flank-then-strike. It moved the gate's numbers hard, in the direction nobody predicted: distinct viable identities fell 6 → 1. The fold roughly doubles effective offense for both sides, and every encounter, opposition and build had been tuned against the superseded −80-only model, so six of seven candidates slid just under the 4-of-6 viability bar. Nothing is masked — every identity still lands its signature wherever its build survives, and that is test-asserted, because masking and sub-viability need opposite fixes. The next slice re-tunes the content (oppositions, encounter geometry, build stats) against the corrected model. N=1 is an honest placeholder, not a target.

restores the gate
02

Provoke / threat & reaction-as-live

Independent medium slices that retire named manifest exclusions: a threat mechanic unlocks the aggro-tank; modeling reactions as live actions unlocks the counter-wall (and reraise-cleric's still-unmodeled sustain identity). Each hardens the gate's teeth; N gain is build-dependent.

+1–2 builds
03

Beyond the job roster

The job roster is being deprioritized. 6 of the 8 authored jobs were measured identities before the move+act fold; post-fold only black-magic. clears the viability bar, pending the content re-tune above. The last two toward the 8-archetype release bar come from new signature prefixes (new jobs — deferred) or the unblocks above; focus shifts to other game systems. Note MP enforcement is the one lever that lowers N — both white-magic. and summon. currently ride unenforced MP.
